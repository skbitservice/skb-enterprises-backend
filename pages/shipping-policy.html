Shipping Policy

How we deliver spare parts and equipment

Home/Shipping Policy

1. Spare Parts Delivery

Spare parts required for service visits are typically carried by our engineers. For specialized or bulk orders, parts are shipped from our central warehouse to the client location within 3-5 business days for metro cities and 5-7 business days for tier-2 and tier-3 cities.

2. Shipping Coverage

We ship spare parts and equipment to all locations across India where our service network is available. This includes all major metros, tier-1 and tier-2 cities. Remote locations may incur additional shipping charges.

3. Shipping Charges

For AMC clients, spare parts shipping is included in the AMC contract. For non-AMC service requests, shipping charges are calculated based on weight, dimensions, and delivery location. Shipping charges will be communicated before order confirmation.

4. Delivery Timeframes

  • Metro Cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, etc.): 1-3 business days
  • Tier-1 Cities: 3-5 business days
  • Tier-2/3 Cities: 5-7 business days
  • Remote Areas: 7-10 business days

5. Tracking

Tracking information is shared via email and SMS once the shipment is dispatched. You can also contact our support team for shipment status updates.

6. Damaged Shipments

If a shipment arrives damaged, please report it within 24 hours of delivery with photographic evidence. We will arrange a replacement at no additional cost for verified damaged shipments.

7. Returns

Incorrect or unwanted parts may be returned within 7 days of delivery in their original packaging. A restocking fee of 15% may apply. Custom-configured parts are non-returnable.
