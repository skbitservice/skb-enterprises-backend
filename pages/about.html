Our Story

SKB Enterprises was founded with a vision to become India's most trusted IT infrastructure services partner. What started as a small team of passionate engineers has grown into a PAN India service network delivering enterprise-grade IT solutions.

We specialize in serving Government, Corporate, Banking, Healthcare, Education, Manufacturing, and Enterprise organizations with comprehensive IT infrastructure management, AMC services, and managed IT support.

Our team of 200+ certified engineers covers 50+ cities across India, ensuring that no matter where your business operates, expert IT support is always within reach.
