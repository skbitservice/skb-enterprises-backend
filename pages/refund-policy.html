Refund Policy

Our refund and cancellation guidelines

Home/Refund Policy

1. Service Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be issued if services were not delivered as per the agreed scope of work. Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of service completion. Refund requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

2. AMC Refund

AMC contracts cancelled within 30 days of activation may be eligible for a full refund minus administrative charges. After 30 days, refunds will be prorated based on services already utilized. Annual AMC payments cancelled mid-term will receive a prorated refund minus a 10% processing fee.

3. Spare Parts Refund

Refunds for spare parts are available within 7 days of installation if the parts are found to be defective. Used or installed parts that are functioning correctly are not eligible for refund. Original packaging and receipts must be presented for parts refund.

4. Advance Payment Refund

Advance payments for scheduled services may be refunded in full if the service is cancelled more than 48 hours before the scheduled date. Cancellations within 48 hours may incur a 25% cancellation fee.

5. Non-Refundable Items

  • Consultation and assessment fees
  • Software licensing charges
  • Third-party product costs already procured
  • Travel and logistics charges for completed visits

6. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed within 7-10 business days. Refunds will be credited to the original payment method. Bank transfer refunds may take an additional 3-5 business days to reflect in your account.

7. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please email support@skbenterprises.com with your service reference number, reason for refund, and supporting details. Our team will review your request within 3 business days.
